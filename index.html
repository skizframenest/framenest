MAKE YOUR COMPARISON USEFUL

What to look for in each video

The opening

Pause after the first few seconds. What question, result, or problem does the video introduce? Write down the exact moment it becomes clear.

The frame

Notice the subject, camera distance, and text placement. Can you follow the idea with sound off? Does the platform interface cover anything important?

The edit

Look for the first cut and each change of shot. Note what each change adds, then choose one editing choice to try in your own work.

Use the notes below each panel to record observations. Download them before clearing the grid or leaving this page.

Questions about the tool

Is FrameNest free?

Yes. This tool requires no signup or paid subscription.

Why won't my video play?

Use a full public TikTok video link. TikTok, your region, your browser settings, or content restrictions may prevent an embed from playing. You can open each video directly using its panel link.

Does it increase video views?

This is a preview and comparison workspace. It does not promise additional reach or generate automated traffic. Videos do not automatically refresh or loop.

What happens to my links?

FrameNest processes links in your browser and does not save them to a FrameNest application server. Loading an embedded video connects your browser to TikTok, which applies its own privacy practices. Notes are kept only in this page and are lost when you rebuild, clear, reload or leave it; downloading saves a text file to your device. Fonts may load from Google. Your appearance preference is saved on this device.
