Privacy at FrameNest

What happens to your links

FrameNest processes pasted links in your browser. It does not send them to a FrameNest application server or save them in a FrameNest account.

When you create a grid, your browser connects to TikTok to load the embedded videos. TikTok handles those requests under its own privacy practices. Fonts may also load from Google. The hosting provider receives the requests needed to serve this website.

What stays on your device

Your light or dark appearance setting is saved in your browser's local storage.

Comparison notes remain in the current page and are lost when you rebuild or clear the grid, reload or leave. A notes export is downloaded to your device; FrameNest does not upload it. Save an export before leaving if you want to keep your notes.

Ads and analytics

FrameNest currently has no advertising or analytics scripts. We will update this page if we add either.

Questions

For technical issues, use the GitHub issue tracker. Issues are public: do not include personal details, confidential notes or private video links.
