FRAMENEST GUIDE & HELP

Why a TikTok video may not play

Check the link first

FrameNest accepts full public video links in this form:

https://www.tiktok.com/@creator/video/1234567890123456789

The address above illustrates the format; it is not a working example video. Short share links need to be opened first. Copy the full video address from your browser, then paste one link per line into FrameNest.

If a link is accepted but the player is empty

  • Use Open on TikTok to check whether the video is available directly.
  • Try another public video. The original may have been removed or made private, or it may have viewing restrictions.
  • Check whether your browser, network or region blocks TikTok embeds. Browser privacy settings or extensions can also affect third-party players. You can test in another browser if you want to compare behavior; you do not need to weaken your privacy settings.

FrameNest relies on TikTok's embedded player. It cannot make an unavailable video public or bypass access restrictions. The tool may accept a correctly formatted link even when its video cannot play.

If the wrong number of videos appears

Choose at least as many panels as links. If you choose more panels than you provide links, FrameNest repeats the links to fill the grid. For three different videos, paste three different links and choose three panels.

If the videos do not start together

Each video has its own playback controls. Start, pause and seek within each player separately. FrameNest does not synchronize playback.

Still stuck? Report a reproducible problem on GitHub Issues. Include your browser and the steps you took; do not post private information or sensitive links.
